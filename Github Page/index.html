What is CRANE?

The EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) places binding cybersecurity obligations on the people who place products with digital elements on the EU market. Meeting those obligations means tracking a lot of evidence: which components ship in each release, which vulnerabilities affect them, how risk was assessed, what was decided at each release, and how long each product will be supported.

CRANE brings that evidence into a single, self-hosted system. Instead of spreadsheets and scattered PDFs, you get a structured, timestamped, exportable record built around the way the CRA is actually written — Article 3 scope, Annex I requirements, Annex II documentation, and the vulnerability-handling and reporting duties.
