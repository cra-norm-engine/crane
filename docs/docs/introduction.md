# CRA Conformity Management

CRA Conformity Management is a self-hosted compliance management platform designed for manufacturers of products with digital elements subject to the **EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847)**. It provides a structured, auditable environment for managing the full lifecycle of CRA obligations — from initial scope determination through to market release and post-market monitoring.

## Purpose

The Cyber Resilience Act establishes mandatory cybersecurity requirements for hardware and software products placed on the EU market. Manufacturers must demonstrate conformity with these requirements through a documented, traceable process. This platform operationalises that process by providing:

- A **product registry** with CRA-relevant metadata and classification
- An **AI-assisted scope evaluation** to determine whether a product falls within the CRA
- **Risk assessment** tracking linked to specific product versions
- A **release gate** that enforces evidence-based readiness checks before a product version is approved for market
- **Substantial modification tracking** in accordance with Article 13(8)
- **Support period** and end-of-support notification management
- **Security update** documentation per Annex I, Part II
- **Certification record** management for third-party assessed products

## Regulatory Scope

This platform addresses obligations applicable to manufacturers under the CRA. It does not cover obligations specific to importers or distributors, nor does it constitute legal advice. Users are responsible for ensuring their compliance processes satisfy the full requirements of the regulation as interpreted in their jurisdiction.

:::important
The CRA applies in phases. The vulnerability handling and support period obligations (Article 13(7), (8), (9)) apply from **11 September 2026**. The full regulation, including conformity assessment requirements, applies from **11 December 2027**. Verify current applicability dates against the official EU Official Journal text.
:::

|---|---|
| Compliance Officer | Scope evaluations, classification decisions, release gate approvals |
| Product Manager | Product and release registration, support period management |
| Cybersecurity Engineer | Risk assessments, substantial change assessment, release gate evidence |
| Legal / Regulatory Affairs | Certification record management, documentation review |
| Developer / DevOps | Local deployment, database migrations, platform configuration |
