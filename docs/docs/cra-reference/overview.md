# EU Cyber Resilience Act — Overview

The **EU Cyber Resilience Act** (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, hereafter "CRA") is a regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council establishing cybersecurity requirements for products with digital elements placed on the EU internal market. It entered into force on 10 December 2024.

:::note
This reference section provides a concise summary of the CRA obligations addressed by this platform. It is not a substitute for the full legislative text or legal counsel. Always refer to the official text published in the EU Official Journal for authoritative guidance.
:::

## Purpose

The CRA addresses the fragmented and insufficient level of cybersecurity across hardware and software products in the EU market. It establishes:

- **Essential cybersecurity requirements** (Annex I) that products must meet before being placed on the market
- **Conformity assessment procedures** (Articles 32–35, Annexes VIII–IX) that manufacturers must follow to demonstrate compliance
- **Post-market obligations** covering vulnerability handling, security updates, and support period disclosure
- **Market surveillance** mechanisms for national authorities and ENISA

## Scope

The CRA applies to **products with digital elements** (PDEs) — hardware and software products that have a direct or indirect logical or physical data connection to a device or network. See [Scope and Exclusions](/cra-reference/scope-and-exclusions) for the full scope definition and excluded categories.

## Application Timeline

| Date | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 10 December 2024 | Regulation enters into force |
| 11 September 2026 | Vulnerability and incident reporting obligations apply (Articles 13(6), 14, 15) |
| 11 December 2027 | Full regulation applies, including conformity assessment requirements |

## Key Obligations for Manufacturers

| Obligation | CRA Reference |
|---|---|
| Design and develop in accordance with essential requirements | Article 13(1); Annex I |
| Conduct and document a cybersecurity risk assessment | Article 13(2); Annex I, Part I; Annex VII |
| Carry out the applicable conformity assessment procedure | Article 32; Annex VIII or IX |
| Draw up the EU Declaration of Conformity | Article 28; Annex V |
| Affix the CE marking | Article 30 |
| Ensure security updates are available for the support period | Article 13(7), (8) |
| Report actively exploited vulnerabilities and incidents | Article 14 |
| Disclose the support period | Article 13(7) |
| Assess whether modifications are substantial | Article 13(8) |

## Enforcement

Compliance is overseen by national market surveillance authorities. ENISA coordinates at EU level. Manufacturers may be subject to fines of up to €15,000,000 or 2.5% of global annual turnover for non-compliance with essential requirements.
